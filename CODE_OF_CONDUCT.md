# Code of conduct

djazairdev brings people together to build open-source software for Algeria. We want every project to be a place where anyone can learn and contribute, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, social and economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, above all with people who are new.
- Respect opinions, experience and languages that differ from yours. Arabic, Tamazight, French and English are all welcome.
- Give and take criticism about the work, never about the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Act for the good of the community, not only for yourself.

## What we don't accept

- Sexualised language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or a phone number, without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In every djazairdev space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events. It also applies elsewhere when someone represents djazairdev, for example from an official account or at an event.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@djazair.dev** with what happened, where and when, and links or screenshots if you have them. Maintainers read every report, reply within 7 days, and keep the reporter's name private. If a report involves a maintainer, they take no part in handling it.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what to do, in steps:

1. **A correction:** a private, written warning that says what was wrong and why. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **A warning:** for a single serious incident or repeated behaviour. No contact with the people involved for a set time, including unsolicited contact with maintainers. Breaking it can lead to a ban.
3. **A temporary ban:** for serious or sustained behaviour. No interaction with djazairdev spaces for a set time.
4. **A permanent ban:** for a pattern of breaking these standards, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people.
